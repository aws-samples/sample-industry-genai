SAMPLE QUESTIONS FOR THE LEGAL CONTRACT ANALYZER
Companion to sample_contract.txt. See SAMPLE_CONTRACT.md for which contract clause
each question targets and what the analyzer is expected to find.

Paste one of these into the "user_question" field alongside the contract text.
Leaving the question blank produces a full executive-summary report instead.


== OPENING THE DEMO ==========================================================

Give me an executive summary of this contract.

What are the key risks in the termination, indemnification, and liability
provisions?

Should we approve this contract?


== LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION =============================================

Is our liability actually capped under this agreement?

What is our maximum financial exposure if there is a data breach?

Is the indemnification balanced between the two parties?

Does our insurance coverage match our contractual exposure?


== DATA PROTECTION AND COMPLIANCE ============================================

Are we GDPR and CCPA compliant if we sign this?

Where is our data being processed, and what protections apply?

What happens to our data if we terminate?

Can the Service Provider use our data for its own purposes?


== COMMERCIAL TERMS ==========================================================

What are our total costs over the full term?

Are there any uncapped or open-ended costs in this agreement?

How easily can we exit this contract?

What notice do we have to give to avoid automatic renewal?


== INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY =====================================================

Who owns the deliverables and the tooling?

What happens to our license to the Provider's tools after termination?


== NEGOTIATION FOCUSED =======================================================

Which three clauses should we push back on first?

Draft the redlines we should request before signing.

What would you change to make this contract acceptable to us as the Client?


== FOLLOW-UP PROBES (good for showing depth) =================================

You flagged the liability cap. Walk me through exactly how it fails.

Compare the termination rights of the two parties side by side.

Which of these risks would you escalate to the General Counsel?
